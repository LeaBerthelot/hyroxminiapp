Hyrox Duo Tracker — install on your iPhone
=============================================

This is a self-contained web app. All your data (activity log, food/alcohol
status) is stored only on your phone, in Safari's local storage — nothing is
sent anywhere, and there is no account or server involved.

RECOMMENDED: host it for free, then add it to your Home Screen
----------------------------------------------------------------
For the full "looks like an app" experience (own icon, no Safari address bar,
works offline), the files need to be served over https rather than opened as
local files. The fastest free options, ~2 minutes each:

  1. GitHub Pages — if you have a GitHub account: create a new repo, upload
     all files in this folder to it, enable Pages in Settings, then open the
     resulting URL on your iPhone.

  2. Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or Vercel — drag this whole folder
     onto the page, get a URL back, open it on your iPhone.

Once you open the URL in Safari on your iPhone:
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add".

You'll get a "Hyrox" icon on your home screen that opens full-screen, like a
real app, and keeps working offline once it's loaded once.

QUICK & DIRTY: no hosting at all
----------------------------------
You can also just AirDrop the whole folder to your iPhone, save it to Files,
then open index.html with Safari (long-press → Share → Safari, or open Files
app and tap it). It'll work and save your data locally, but iOS may treat
"Add to Home Screen" as a plain bookmark rather than a full standalone app
icon in this mode — good enough to try it today, worth the 2-minute hosting
step once you know you'll keep using it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
----------------------------------
index.html, app.js, chart.umd.min.js  — the app itself
manifest.json, sw.js, icon-*.png       — home-screen icon + offline support

Your training program (Sept 1 → Dec 6, phased 3→4→5 sessions/week, Boxing
always Tuesday, Hyrox always Friday, taper race week) is generated automatically
by the app — nothing to configure.
